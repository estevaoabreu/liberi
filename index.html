About Liberi

Liberi is an advanced medical telemetry monitor built to give parents peace of mind.

By reading smart hardware data over Web Serial or wireless nodes, the application constantly evaluates real-time vital signs against safe physiological boundaries.

If readings spike abnormal or concerning, system push notifications will alert you instantly to ensure your child remains completely protected.
